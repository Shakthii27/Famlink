Why FamLink

A platform built with
heart and purpose

We go beyond basic matching — we understand personalities, values, and emotional needs to create lasting family bonds.

💫

Emotional Matching

Our intelligent compatibility system aligns children's interests, personalities, and aspirations with parent values and lifestyle — reducing age-based bias.

🔒

Secure & Private

Role-based access control ensures that child information is only visible to authorized parties. No direct contact without agency approval.

📋

Full Process Tracking

Track your application through every stage — from submission and background checks to counselling sessions and final approval.

🧠

Counselor Insights

Psychologists and social workers document each child's emotional needs, trauma history, and behavioral notes to help parents prepare.

📚

Educational Resources

Access expert-curated guides on bonding with older children, understanding trauma, and navigating the emotional journey of adoption.

🌱

Post-Adoption Support

Our follow-up program monitors child wellbeing during the transition phase, offering ongoing counsellor access and parent support.

The Process

Your adoption journey,
step by step

A transparent, supported process from your first profile to welcoming a child home.

1

Create Profile

Share your lifestyle, values, and parenting preferences to build your family profile.

2

Home Study

A counselor visits and evaluates your home environment. HCR is prepared and reviewed.

3

Browse & Match

Explore child profiles and receive compatibility suggestions based on shared values.

4

Apply

Submit your adoption application with all required legal documentation.

5

Counselling

Attend mandatory counselling sessions to prepare emotionally for adoption.

6

Welcome Home

Agency approves the match. Your new family chapter begins with our ongoing support.
